    Listening help

    Listening

    Choose a team and, when one is listed, a game. Automatic plays the published game feed first, then the team’s network, its backup connection and affiliate stations. If a feed fails, Homecall moves to the next one, says so, and starts it at 0 seconds; check alignment with your TV after any switch. Source lets you pick one yourself, for example when a station carries the wrong program.

    Live network channel. Game coverage depends on the broadcaster.

    Call ahead of the TV? Open Match my TV, pause to match, then resume when the picture catches up.

    Call behind the TV? Reduce added delay. At 0 seconds, pause your TV until the audio catches up. Reducing delay skips audio forward; adding delay repeats earlier audio.

    After commercials, buffering or a phone interruption, check alignment again. Background and locked-screen playback are not yet verified.
